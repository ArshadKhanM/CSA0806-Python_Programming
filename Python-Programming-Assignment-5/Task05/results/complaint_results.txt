Interpretation: the hand-authored labels are a small, synthetic reference set, not a held-out clinical or city-wide evaluation. The simple lexicon handles direct polarity and one-token negation, but misses sarcasm, scope, double negation, and domain-specific meaning. The extractive summary retains salient source text; its coverage measure counts a lexicon word and does not establish factual quality. A model should be considered only after a representative, privacy-reviewed multilingual evaluation shows a useful improvement.
